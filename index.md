# Privacy Policy for Nova Dash

**Last updated:** October 23, 2026

Nova Dash ("the App") is developed and published by the developer. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the App.

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect any personal data.** 

Nova Dash is an offline, single-player game. It does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information. 

Specifically, we do **not** collect:
- Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
- Location data
- Device identifiers for tracking
- Usage analytics or crash reports
- Contacts, photos, or files
- Payment or financial information

## Data Stored Locally

The App stores only game-related data locally on your device, such as:
- Your high score and best distance
- The highest level reached (for "continue from last level")
- Total coins and total runs
- Game settings (music/sound volume and toggles)
- First-run tutorial state

This data is saved in your device's app-private storage (`user://novadash.cfg`). It never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

## Third-Party Services

Nova Dash does **not** use any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics tools, or trackers.

## Children’s Privacy

Nova Dash does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since no data is collected at all, the App is safe for users of all ages.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via the contact details provided on the Google Play Store listing for Nova Dash.
